Client portal — the same product, four destinations

The client role has eleven screens on the phone and needs four on the web: today, progress, sessions, payments. It is deliberately the calmest thing in the system — a client opens it to answer one question, and every extra control is a way to fail them.

The restraint is the design

A client’s web app could show everything the trainer sees. It shows four things. Their sessions left, whether they showed up, what they owe, and what they are lifting today — because those are the four questions they actually open it to answer, and the product promise is that they are never sold anything here.
