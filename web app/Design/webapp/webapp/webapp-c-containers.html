Containers — where things go, and how deep they are allowed to stack

Seven components that decide the shape of every screen. The rule underneath all of them: a sheet becomes a panel when the thing behind it matters, and a modal when it does not. Payments, sessions and add-client are panels because you decide them by looking at the ledger, the week or the roster. Archive is a modal, because nothing behind it helps and it must be answered.

SUPERSEDED · 23 Aug 2026 — the web app is ONLINE-ONLY

This page was written offline-first. That decision has been reversed for the web: the web app writes every response straight to the server — no browser storage, no write queue, no sync. Offline-first remains the architecture of the phone (app/) and of nothing else.

So wherever this page draws or argues for an offline banner, a synced / queued pill, a “held on this device” count, or the sync-queue screen, that chrome is not built on the web. The reasoning around it is kept, because the rest of each finding depends on it — only the offline half is void.

Authority: CLAUDE.md at the repo root, and web app/web/AGENTS.md. Do not re-derive the offline behaviour from this page.

The 2026 pass — the panel arrives

30 Aug 2026

The overlay layer ARRIVES now instead of appearing mid-paint: the panel slides in from its edge, the modal grows from centre, the palette drops, the menu pops from its origin, the scrim fades — 180–240ms on the entrance curve, and gone entirely under prefers-reduced-motion. backdrop-filter itself is never animated (§24’s rule — the browser recomputes the blur every frame), which is exactly why these animate transform and opacity instead. The line §24 drew in research — glass floats, content stays flat — is where the platform vendors landed: Apple’s Liquid Glass ships exactly that split across iOS 26 and macOS Tahoe, and the pass extends our glass to the two floaters it missed, the row menu and the toast.

Modal

Stable

A question that must be answered before anything else happens. There are four in the whole product, all destructive, and each one has the same job: say what happens to the money and whether it can be undone.

The four questions every modal answers

guidelines
QuestionIn this example
What exactly is being changed?The title names the person: Archive Meera Krishnan? Never “this item”.
What happens to the money and the history?“34 logged sessions and ₹68,000 of payment history stay exactly where they are and keep counting towards your GST turnover.”

Can it be undone?Archive is reversible — and where an action is not, the modal says so in those words and the confirm button carries the object name.

RuleWhy
