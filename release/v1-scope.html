Not in v1

Designed or half-built, and deliberately held back.

  • NEXT
    Client portal. Home, workout player, plan, progress, answering assessments, shared notes, account. Mostly built. Waits for the consent screen, a lawyer's answer on who holds a client's data, and the client-side security tests.
  • LATER
    Sign in with Google. Not built. Open question: a fallback when WhatsApp fails, with the phone number added later, or a way to use the product without giving a number.
  • LATER
    Blood pressure and resting heart rate. Hidden from the assessment catalogue for v1. Visceral fat and "did anything hurt" stay in.
  • LATER
    GST summary. Most independent trainers are under the registration threshold. Built; hidden for v1.
  • LATER
    Offline tolerance on the web. Gyms mostly have Wi-Fi, so v1 assumes a connection.
  • OUT
    Phone app. The Expo trainer and client apps, offline sync included.
  • OUT
    Team coaching. Team roster, invite a coach, teammates' clients, shared library, activity log, owner revenue roll-up. Built; hidden for v1.
  • OUT
    Workspaces. One trainer coaching in two workspaces with separate books. Built; hidden for v1.
  • OUT
    Gym platform and admin console. Ring 2. Designed, not built.
  • OUT
    WhatsApp nudges from a platform number. Nudges stay as wa.me links from the trainer's own number. The platform number sends OTP only.
  • OUT
    AI chat. Spec exists, pricing deferred.
  • OUT
    Photos of any kind. No profile photo, progress photos or exercise GIFs. There is no image store, and the exercise artwork is unlicensed.

Must clear before release

Not features, but nobody can use the features until these land. From WEB_LAUNCH.md.

  • MUST-1
    WhatsApp OTP delivery. Nothing can sign in off a dev machine. Needs a WhatsApp Business provider, Meta business verification and an approved authentication template. Those are queues: start them first.
  • MUST-2
    Web sends X-InclineYou-Client: web. Otherwise the browser holds an unrevocable 7-day JWT and sign-out does not sign out.
  • MUST-16
    30-day trial clock and Free-tier cap. Must exist before the first real signup, with read-only degrade on expiry. The Plan & trial screen is part of this.
  • MUST-3·4
    Dockerfiles and web CI. Nothing is deployable today.
  • MUST-19
    Hide what v1 does not ship. Team, workspace menu, portal routes, GST tab, BP and heart rate, shared notes and "send to client", each behind a flag.
  • MUST-20
    Trainer data export. CSV of clients, packs, payments and sessions.
  • MUST-21
    Trainer takes an assessment. Write path for readings and answers, and the screen to enter them.
  • MUST-5–9
    Hardening. @Valid on nine controllers, fail-fast API URL, security headers, Server Action origins, a real rotatable JWT secret.
  • DAY 31
    Razorpay rail and GST invoices. MUST-17·18. Due when the first trials expire, not at launch.
