Home

Twelve states, a full navigation drawer, and a tap map for every clickable thing on the screen. Built on a teardown of twelve trainer platforms, which produced one clear finding: a trainer's home is a to-do list, not a report. Every serious platform converged on a “needs attention” module at the top, and not one of them leads with a chart.

01 · The screen

Default — a full day

Seven modules, in the order a trainer needs them: what's next, where the day stands, who needs chasing, the schedule, the money, and what's happened since. No greeting — nobody in the category ships one, and it would cost the most valuable 60px on the screen.

03 · Navigation

Four tabs, and a drawer for everything weekly-or-rarer

NN/g's study is unambiguous: hidden navigation cuts discoverability by more than half and raises perceived task difficulty by 21%. So the rule isn't “no hamburger” — it's nothing daily goes behind it. Four tabs cover the daily surfaces; the drawer holds the weekly and the once-ever.

05 · The drawer

Everything that isn't daily

GroupItemsWhy here and not in the bar
HeaderAvatar · name · client count · gym · role switchMaterial 3's documented drawer profile block. Tapping it opens profile & business settings.
BusinessPayments (2) · Packages · ReportsFirst, deliberately. Every competitor locks money to a desktop browser. Badge counts overdue.
TrainingPrograms · Exercises (873)Weekly work. Also reachable contextually from a client's profile — NN/g: never make the drawer the only path. Weekly slots deliberately isn't here — it's template state for the calendar, so it lives under Diary, and the drawer must not be a second route to something a tab already owns.
ClientsNudges (5) · AdherenceRoster-wide views. The per-client route is the Clients tab.
AppSettings · HelpSet once. Support routes to WhatsApp in India.
FooterSign out · versionVersion is tappable to copy — support asks for it every time.

Why not a “More” tab

Apple says outright to avoid overflow tabs. Peloton — a company with real telemetry — renamed theirs to “Experiences” because “More” tells the user nothing. The hamburger is the More; shipping both is two doors to the same room.

Badges only where a number drives action

Overdue payments, unread nudges. Never badge Settings. A permanently badged drawer trains people to ignore badges.

The hamburger's real risk

NN/g measured it: hidden nav is used 57% of the time vs 86% for a visible/hidden combo, and an icon tucked next to a logo hits 44% usage against 89% for a weighted, labelled trigger. So: real tap area, separated from the title, and every drawer destination reachable a second way.

iOS gets the same IA, a different container

No hamburger on iOS — it's an avatar button top-right opening a sheet with the identical grouped list. Same information architecture, native container. On iPad, .tabViewStyle(.sidebarAdaptable).

06 · What the teardown changed

Twelve platforms

FindingEvidenceWhat we did
Home is a triage board, not a reportTrueCoach, CoachRx and Trainerize all converge on “Needs Attention”. Nobody leads with a chart.Attention module sits third, above the schedule — after only the live card and the rail.
Most coach apps have no home at allHevy Coach mobile is Clients + Chats. Everfit, FitBudd, Kahunas, FITR the same. Exercise.com has no trainer app.The category has conceded the phone. That's the whole opening.
Vague statuses beat by explicit thresholdsCoachRx: “missed 2 in a row”, “below 50% compliance”. Trainerize: “not active in 7 days”, adjustable.Alert copy states the number: “9 days”, “11 days”, “2 sessions” — never “low compliance”.
Nobody greets by nameNot WHOOP, Oura, Strava, TrueCoach or CoachRx.Date and session count instead. Costs one line, not a hero.
Money is absent from mobileOnly FitBudd surfaces pending payments. Trainerize locks all Stripe functions to web.Pending in the stat rail, money card on home, Payments at the top of the drawer.
Banners should be conditional, not chromeWHOOP stacks banners only when they have a job — alarm set, journal incomplete, activity detected.Offline, sync queue and the profile meter all appear only when true.
Segment the day by delivery modeTrueCoach segments in-person / dual / remote — the only one that does.Floor / Remote / Done chips with live counts.
Feeds need inline reactions to be worth havingCoachRx puts comments and reactions in the activity feed.Activity rows are tappable to the thing itself; reactions land with the client screen.
