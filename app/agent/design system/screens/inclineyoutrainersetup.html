01 · Before anything

Tell them what's coming

The most-named onboarding complaint across every platform reviewed was not form length — it was surprise. One Exercise.com trainer: “I wasn't made aware of all the things they would require from me before they could launch my platform.” A pre-flight screen costs nothing and prevents the worst review you'll get. Note there's no name in the greeting — we don't know it yet. All OTP gave us is a phone number.

04 · Certifications and languages

Where the category is quietly dishonest

Four of eight platforms collect certifications. None of them verify any of it. India has no licensing requirement for personal trainers either — no mandated certificate, no statutory register, no protected title. So we collect what trainers actually hold, and we say plainly that we haven't checked it.

05 · Getting paid

Format only — and the design has to admit it

We check that a UPI ID looks like a UPI ID. We don't call a payment provider, so we can't confirm the account exists or that it's the trainer's. A single wrong letter still passes. That's a real risk to a trainer's money, so the screen stops being a form and becomes a read-back.

Steal: the fake client

Trainerize seeds “Timmy Explorer” on account creation. It breaks the deadlock where a trainer won't invite a real client until they understand the product, and can't understand the product without one. Best single idea in the category.

Avoid: relocating friction

Kahunas has a 30-second signup and then needs an Apple Developer account, a Google Play org account and possibly a D-U-N-S number — discovered after subscribing. Friction wasn't removed, it was moved past the commitment point.

Avoid: desktop-gating the trainer

Trainerize blocks signup on mobile entirely; Everfit recommends desktop for profile editing. The incumbents have collectively conceded the phone — which for an India-first product is the whole opening.

The benchmark

Hevy Coach: dashboard is the first screen, Add Client is on it, zero mandatory steps in between. We match the feel by deferring, not by dropping.
