Train X · Screens 07–16 · Drawer destinations

Behind the drawer

Eighteen states covering every interaction in the navigation drawer — the profile block, the mode switch, all seven destinations, and sign out — each with a tap map. It opens with a correction: the drawer we designed before the Money tab existed had two destinations that the Money tab now owns. Leaving them would break the rule this system already set for itself, so the first thing on this page is the drawer with those two removed.

01 · The drawer, corrected

Two destinations removed, and why

When the drawer was designed, Payments and Packages had nowhere else to live. The Money tab now contains both — the ledger, the chase list, and the packs you sell. The Clients screen set the rule out loud: the drawer must not be a second route to something a tab already owns, which is why Weekly slots moved into Diary. The same rule applies here, so Payments and Packages are gone and the remaining seven regroup into Training · Growth · App.

02 · Training

A template library and an exercise library, and the difference matters

A program is a template; assigning it copies it onto a client. That one sentence prevents the worst bug in this category — editing a template and silently changing a plan somebody is halfway through. TrueCoach and Trainerize both got there; it is worth saying on the screen.

03 · Growth

Reports, adherence, and the nudges that come out of them

Trainerize's metrics are the right ones — “Sign-ins per week”, “Workouts per week”, Exercise Compliance — and its scores are “calculated every Sunday night”. Weekly recalculation means a client who stopped on Tuesday looks fine until the weekend. Train X computes adherence live, on the phone, from local data.

06 · The teardown

What each platform does with these surfaces

SurfaceBest in classWhat Train X takes — or refuses
Exercise libraryHevy. “400+ high-quality exercises”, filters for equipment and muscle targets plus search. An exercise page carries a demo animation, heaviest weight, projected 1RM, best set and session volume, total reps, performance history and step-by-step instructions. Custom exercises take an image or video, name, equipment, primary and secondary muscles and a type — and “once a custom exercise has been saved… you cannot edit the type”Nearly all of it. The three records a coach quotes out loud, the two filters, and the immutable log type — with the reason stated on screen instead of buried in a help centre. Added: a Yours chip, because the exercise you invented is the one you hunt for
Program buildingTrueCoach and Trainerize. Program → week → day → exercise, with sets, reps, rest, tempo, RPE, notes and supersets, reusable as templatesThe hierarchy and the fields. Refused: building a twelve-week program on a phone. Train X shows, assigns and edits on mobile; authoring from scratch is a desktop job and pretending otherwise produces a bad program
Engagement & complianceTrainerize. “Sign-ins per week” and “Workouts per week” as averages over the prior week; Exercise Compliance from scheduled workouts completed; Nutrition Compliance over three weeks. “Scores are calculated every Sunday night”, and coaches read them on the web accountThe metric definitions. Refused twice: weekly recalculation, which hides a client who stopped on Tuesday until the weekend — and web-only, which is useless to somebody standing on a gym floor
Nudges & automationEverfit and Trainerize both automate messages on triggersThe triggers. Refused: automation as the default. Every rule here starts on Ask me first, and the send window and frequency cap are not settings
Settings & supportNobody in particularWhatsApp as the first support row, three languages, and an FAQ whose first entry answers the question our own architecture creates — where do I withdraw my money

What Hevy got right

An exercise page that answers “what's my best?” before it answers “how do I do it?”. And an immutable log type, which is the sort of constraint that only comes from having shipped.

What the coaching platforms got wrong

Compliance is computed weekly and shown on desktop. Both decisions make the number arrive too late to act on, which is the only reason to compute it at all.

What we deliberately skipped

No program authoring from scratch on a phone. No nutrition compliance — we don't track food, so a score would be fiction. No in-app chat: WhatsApp already won, and a second inbox is a second thing to ignore.

What India adds

Support in Tamil, English and Hindi over WhatsApp. A gym-cut setting with a from-today-only rule. Self-declared certifications, said plainly. And an FAQ entry on GST, because the ₹20 lakh line arrives without warning.
