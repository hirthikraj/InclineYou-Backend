Train X · Screen 04 · Clients · FR-1 · FR-5

Clients

Fourteen states and a tap map for every clickable thing on the screen, built on a teardown of how nine coaching platforms organise a roster. They agree on one thing and miss one thing. They all agree the list has to surface who needs you — Trainerize computes it, Everfit segments it, TrueCoach sorts by it. And every single one of them puts the money on another screen. For a trainer selling ten-session packs for cash and UPI, who owes you is the roster.

01 · The roster

One list, sorted by who needs you

The default order is not alphabetical. Alphabetical is a lookup order, and lookup is what search is for — a trainer opening this tab is far more often asking “who do I have to chase?” than “where is Ananya?”. So the list opens on Needs attention, pinned above everyone else, and A–Z is one tap away.

02 · Finding someone

Search is a screen, not a filter

The search field is on the roster permanently rather than behind an icon. On Home search is an icon because Home isn't a list; here, finding a person is the second reason anyone opens the tab. Tapping it pushes a full search screen — Material 3's full-screen search view — because results span clients, groups and programs, and those can't be rendered inside a roster.

03 · Acting on a client

Three ways in, and none of them is the only way

NN/g's finding on contextual swipe is blunt: “burying key actions behind a contextual swipe prevents users from discovering them”. So the rule here is that swipe is never the only path. Every action on the swipe is also in the long-press menu, and everything in the long-press menu is also on the client's own screen.

What everyone got right

Every serious platform computes who needs attention rather than asking the trainer to remember. Trainerize's auto-tags are the best version of this idea in the category.

What everyone got wrong

Money is on another screen in all nine. For a trainer selling packs for cash and UPI, “who owes me” is not a report — it is the roster, and it is the first thing they scan.

What we deliberately skipped

No compliance percentage on the row. A number like 68% reads as a grade and starts arguments; “no workout in 9 days” is the same fact and it names the action.

What India adds

Batches, not groups. UPI and cash side by side. And WhatsApp as the messaging layer — one chat per client, never a broadcast, because a group message about money loses clients.

07 · Rules this screen sets

Things a dev can hold us to

RuleWhy
Roster paints from local storage before any network callFR-8. A roster that spins on open is a roster you stop opening
Segment counts come from local dataA chip that says “5” and then changes to “3” after sync is worse than no count
Attention rules are computed client-side and are visible in SettingsThe trainer must be able to change “quiet” from 7 days to 14. Trainerize makes those thresholds adjustable; so do we
Never more than two swipe actionsNN/g. Beyond two, nothing is discoverable and the row is hidden
Every swipe action exists in the long-press menu tooNN/g: swipe must not be the only path to an action
Bulk message opens one chat per clientMoney is private. A broadcast about fees costs clients
The A–Z rail only renders when sorted A–ZAn index into an urgency-sorted list points at nothing
Selection mode never shifts a row horizontallyThe checkbox occupies the avatar's slot, so the eye keeps its place
